This is EVE's briefing on what Chris is building. Chris maintains it by hand. Everything in it is public and EVE may repeat any of it; nothing confidential belongs here. Lists run newest first.

## Right now

- **Beacons is the main build.** Live noise-compliance and acoustic monitoring for venues and live events, at beaconsio.com. Chris is co-founder and CTO and built the whole platform himself: the iPhone app with on-device signal processing, the web dashboard, the cloud backend, and dedicated sensor hardware. It has been used on stage by a professional symphony orchestra. It is featured on this site's homepage. If a visitor is curious about anything live-sound related, suggest they check out beaconsio.com.
- **This site was rebuilt in October 2026**: a full test suite, a hardened Content Security Policy, and EVE moved to DeepSeek V4.1 Flash with streaming replies. The site gets rebuilt with each new model generation.
- **Byte Mail's source is public.** The production code behind Byte is at github.com/Nxtvanhalen/byte-mail: Bun + Hono, DeepSeek with model routing, Redis threading, a safety layer for sensitive topics, and 560+ tests. Byte is still free at byte@firstlyte.co; outside users found it on their own and use it every day.

## Featured work (homepage "Selected Work", in this order)

1. **The Underground** — Live, browser game. Run a small underground music venue in cyberpunk-noir: book bands, keep the crew right, dodge incidents, balance the books. Twenty years of running venues turned into a piece of working software, built end to end with agents. https://underground-venue-manager.onrender.com
2. **Byte** — Live, free community project (not a startup pitch). An AI assistant that lives entirely in email: write to byte@firstlyte.co and get a thoughtful reply in under 30 seconds. No app, no login, no account. Handles attachments, remembers threads, routes to the right model. https://firstlyte.co · source: https://github.com/Nxtvanhalen/byte-mail
3. **Beacons** — Live. SPL and acoustic monitoring for live events: cloud logging, real-time analysis, predictive insight. Protects patrons and staff; keeps neighbors and regulators happy. Operated under CLB Consulting, Portland, Oregon. https://beaconsio.com
4. **Fuel Estimator** — Live, free. A tour-bus fuel-cost calculator for tour managers, priced per state: routes, miles, price per gallon, so budgets hold up before the wheels roll. https://fuel-estimator.onrender.com
